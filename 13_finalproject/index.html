<div class="textcontainer"> <p class="margin"></p> <h3>Final Project: AUTOMATED JEWELRY BOX</h3> <h4>Demo Video:</h4> <br></br> <p> [INSERT] Video of final thing & YouTube link </p> <video width="640" height="480" controls> <source src="360vid_cut.mp4" type="video/mp4"> </video> <h4> Inspiration for the Project </h4> <img src="./jewelryOG.jpg" alt="CNC Milling" style="width:300px"> <p> As someone who loves wearing jewelery (and owns a lot of it), I have a couple jewelry organizers in my dorm. This project was inspired by one of these organizers (pictured below). For this jewelry stand, one has to manually rotate the hooks go through the options and choose the desired jewlery. I wanted to make a way to automate this process. I designed and build a jewelry organizer, that has automatically rotating hooks so that someone can browse through their options. A user interface is also incorporated where the person can user can also select the specific hook to retrieve their desired jewelry. </p> <h4>Timeline of pictures from my first prototype to the final box: </h4> <img src="./woodprototype.jpg" alt="Wood prototype" style="width:300px"> <img src="./MVP.jpg" alt="MVP Project" style="width:300px"> <img src="./FINAL.jpg" alt="Picture of final jewelry holder" style="width:300px"> <h4>List of Materials and Tools Used:</h4> <ul> <li>Black acrylic panes</li> <li>Clear acrylic panes</li> <li>Hinges</li> <li>Super Glue</li> <li>Tape</li> </ul> <h4>Electronics Used:</h4> <ul> <li>Pin-Pin Wires</li> <li>Pin-Socket Wires</li> <li>Rotary Encoder</li> <li>OLED Screen</li> <li>Stepper Motor</li> <li>A4988 Stepper Motor Driver</li> <li>XIAO ESP32 Microcontroller</li> <li>12V Power Supply</li> </ul> <h4>Fabrication Process</h4> <p> The jewlery organizer has three main components: the rotating hooks, the user interface and the outer frame.</p> <h4>Rotating Hooks</h4> <img src="./hook prototypes.jpg" alt="Picture of final jewelry holder" style="width:300px"> <p> For the design of the hook, I used a similar design to the hooks in the original jewelery organizer. There was a main body with a hole in the top center for the stepper motor and another hole at the top for a magnet. There is also a small hole at the side to insert a set screw to hold the piece in place when attached to the motor. <div style="display:flex; gap:30px;"> <div style="text-align:center;"> <p>Main Body</p> <img src="./mainbody.png" alt="main body" style="width:200px"> </div> <div style="text-align:center;"> <p>Hooks</p> <img src="./hooks.png" alt="hooks" style="width:300px"> </div> <div style="text-align:center;"> <p>Both Parts Together</p> <img src="./assembly.png" alt="assembly file" style="width:300px"> </div> </div> <p> Each individual hook has 4 grooves to hold the necklaces or pieces of jewelry. In different iterations of this piece, I used either 4 or 8 hooks. I also played around with detachable arms or having the arms being apart of the main body. In the end, I went with 8 hooks with the hook arms being a part of the main body as this was better for stability and the aesthetic I was going for. </p> <h4>User Interface</h4> <div class="flexrow"> <video width="640" height="480" controls> <source src="drawingX.mp4" type="video/mp4"> </video> </div> <p> To get the structure to rotate to a specific hook, I went through different iterations of positioning. I first used an interface with 4 buttons as described in <a href="https://zariaferguson.github.io/PS70ZFerguson/07_outputs/index.html"> Week 7</a>. I then incorporated a rotary encoder and OLED screen that allows the user to scroll through the menu of options and choose their desired hook (<a href="https://zariaferguson.github.io/PS70ZFerguson/10_machine/index.html">Week 10</a>). My final version of this involved incoporating the stepper motor with the rotary encoder and the OLED screen. When the code runs, a menu of 8 hooks appears on the OLED screen for the user to scroll through by rotating the rotary encoder on the side of the box. When the user clicks the button on the rotary encoder, a screen comes up that reads "Hook [i] Moving..." where [i] would be the number of the hook that was selected. The system rotates to the specific hook. Pressing the button again afterwards allows the system to spin continuously again. <p> In order to spin to the correct hook, the code records the current position and the target position of the selected hook and calculates the difference in order to determine whether to spin forwards or backwards to get to the hook. </p> <p> Here is the code incorporating the rotary encoder, the OLED and the stepper motor for positioning of the hooks: </p> <div class="flexrow"> <a id="btn" href="motor_rotaryencoder_OLED.zip" download>Final Project Code </a> </div> <h4>Outer Frame</h4> <p> The outer frame of this box was made by using black acrylic panes for the walls, the top face and the bottom face with a clear acrylic pane for the door. These pieces were designed as finger joint pieces from https://makercase.com/ and then laser cut. For the top face, I also cut through holes that match the dimensions of the stepper motor so that the motor can sit flush on the structure. Small circular pieces were laser cut from the clear acrylic pane to make the door knob. Two hinges were attached to the side of the box to faciliate the opening and closing or the jewelry box. Super glue was used to attach all the pieces together. </p> <img src="./all black.jpg" alt="Picture of final jewelry holder" style="width:300px"> <img src="./clearandblack.jpg" alt="Picture of final jewelry holder" style="width:300px"> <p> The upper frame of this box was 3D printed leaving openings for the OLED screen and wire connections. </p> <img src="./uppercasing.png" alt="Picture of final jewelry holder" style="width:300px"> <p> Electronics </p> <p> Here is the circuit incorporating the rotary encoder, OLED screen and stepper motor </p> <img src="./finalcircuit.jpg" alt="Picture of final jewelry holder" style="width:300px"> <p> Here is the final circuit after being soldered with the wires being extended to connect to the electronic components of the box. </p> <img src="./solderboard.jpg" alt="Picture of final jewelry holder" style="width:300px"> <strong><h4>FINAL PRODUCT</h4></strong> <p> The final product is an automated jewelry organizer. It features 8 hooks attached to a main body, each with 4 grooves, that is hung from a stepper motor. A rotary encoder attached to the side is also featured for the user to scroll through the menu and choose their desired jewelry. The casing on top houses some of the electronics and displays an OLED screen that has 8 options for the user to choose from on a scrollable menu. </p> [INSERT COLLAGE VIDEO OF ALL THE COMPONENTS] <div class="flexrow"> <video width="640" height="480" controls> <source src="drawingX.mp4" type="video/mp4"> </video> </div> <p> For future iterations, I would envision making a cleaner housing for the electronics that sits to the side of the box and includes a mirror.
